Word by word

How it works: GloVe word vectors (a close cousin of word2vec, trained on Wikipedia and news text) give each word its position. A sentiment direction is found by comparing clearly positive and negative words, and emotions by closeness to groups of emotion words. Spanish words are translated with a small built-in dictionary first. It reads word choice, not sarcasm or full context, so treat it as a fun first impression.
